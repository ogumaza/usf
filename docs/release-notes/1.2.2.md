In this release, `usfrip` adds five Toad Town tracks to Paper Mario's set and names three others after the places where the game plays them. Rip Paper Mario again (see below). usf rips Nintendo 64 music into USF, the PSF-family format for the Nintendo 64, and plays it by running the game's code on an emulated console. The release comes with a ripper (`usfrip`), a renderer (`usfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `usf-1.2.2-win-x64.zip` | `usfrip` and `usfplay` for 64-bit Windows |
| `foo_input_usfplay-1.2.2.fb2k-component` | the foobar2000 component, for 64-bit and 32-bit foobar2000 v2 |
| `xmp-usfplay-1.2.2.zip` | the XMPlay plugin, for XMPlay 3.8.2 or later |

The programs run on Windows 10 or later and require no runtime DLLs. To install the component, open the file with foobar2000, or add it under Preferences › Components. To install the XMPlay plugin, unzip `xmp-usfplay-1.2.2.zip` and put `xmp-usfplay.dll` in the XMPlay folder, or in a folder inside it. Then restart XMPlay. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## Rip Paper Mario again

Rips of Paper Mario made with 1.2.1 or an earlier release have no tracks for the music outside five buildings of Toad Town. Rip the game again with this release. Against a rip made with 1.2.1, the new set has five more tracks, right after the ten Toad Town tracks. Every later track therefore gets a number 5 higher, in its file name and in its `track` tag. Apart from that and three new titles, the other tracks don't change.

## Changes since 1.2.1

### Paper Mario's Toad Town

- Three tracks have new titles, and new file names to match. "Toad Town, mix 4" is now "Toad Town, the pipe to the Tunnels". "Toad Town, mix 5" is now "Toad Town, Minh T.'s Garden". "Toad Town, mix 8" is now "Toad Town, Rowf's Badge Shop". The tracks keep their numbers and sound the same.
- Five new tracks play the music outside the five buildings that already have a track, such as Tayce T.'s House. Their titles name the building after "outside", such as "Toad Town, outside Tayce T.'s House".
- In some places in town, Toad Town's song adds extra instruments. The game brings them in only once the song has left its intro, and fades them in over 1.5 s. In these tracks they therefore come in when the song reaches its loop. The game does the same when a mix starts during the intro. Inside five of the buildings, such as Tayce T.'s House, the extra instruments play at full level and the rest of the band plays quieter. Outside those buildings, and by the pipe to the Tunnels, Minh T.'s Garden and Rowf's Badge Shop, the extra instruments play quieter and the band plays at full level.
