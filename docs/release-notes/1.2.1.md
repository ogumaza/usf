In this release, `usfrip` rips Paper Mario's Normal Battle with the room effect that the game's first battles have, and adds six Normal Battle tracks with the game's two other effects. Rip Paper Mario again (see below). usf rips Nintendo 64 music into USF, the PSF-family format for the Nintendo 64, and plays it by running the game's code on an emulated console. The release comes with a ripper (`usfrip`), a renderer (`usfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|

| `foo_input_usfplay-1.2.1.fb2k-component` | the foobar2000 component, for 64-bit and 32-bit foobar2000 v2 |
| `xmp-usfplay-1.2.1.zip` | the XMPlay plugin, for XMPlay 3.8.2 or later |

The programs run on Windows 10 or later and require no runtime DLLs. To install the component, open the file with foobar2000, or add it under Preferences › Components. To install the XMPlay plugin, unzip `xmp-usfplay-1.2.1.zip` and put `xmp-usfplay.dll` in the XMPlay folder, or in a folder inside it. Then restart XMPlay. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## Rip Paper Mario again

Rips of Paper Mario made with 1.2.0 or an earlier release play the four Normal Battle tracks with the echo. Rip the game again with this release. The new set has six more tracks, right after the four Normal Battle tracks. Every later track therefore gets a number 6 higher, in its file name and in its `track` tag. Apart from that, the other tracks don't change.

## Changes since 1.2.0

### Paper Mario's Normal Battle

- The battle song sets no room effect. It keeps the effect of the field song that played before the battle. The game's areas with enemies are split almost evenly between three effects: the small room, the echo and the big room. The game's first battles, on the roads around Goomba Village and Koopa Village, follow a field song with the small room.
- The four Normal Battle tracks keep their names and now start with the small room. Before, they started with the echo. They therefore sound different now.
- Six new tracks play versions 1 to 3 of Normal Battle with the two other effects: three with the echo and three with the big room. Their titles end with "echo" and "big room". These are the versions that an encounter starts, depending on who strikes first.
